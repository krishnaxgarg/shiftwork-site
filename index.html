Why SHIFT

Three things keep students from working part-time.

Stigma

Families often see a part-time job as a step down. We're making it something students are proud of.

Safety and trust

Students and parents need to know who they're working for. Every employer on SHIFT is verified, and so is every student.

No clear place to start

Good part-time shifts are scattered and hard to find. SHIFT puts them in one place.

How it works

From sign-up to your first shift.
